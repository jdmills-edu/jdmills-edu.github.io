Two different reasons for "CARRIER: WAITING"

Nov 2026
Problem

The FM screen of a handheld SDR firmware sat on "waiting for carrier" with no audio, on two separate occasions.

What worked

The first time, reading the firmware source showed the screen was locked to a frequency below the onboard LR2021's floor, a job the firmware hands to an external RTL-SDR that wasn't plugged in. The second time, the console showed no dongle registered at all. A reboot and USB root-port resets didn't help, and the schematic showed the USB-A power switch's enable pin tied to 5 V, so no software can cut the dongle's power.

Result

Retuning fixed the first case. The second needed a physical unplug and replug, after which HF came straight back.

Lesson A bus-powered dongle that hangs stays hung until VBUS drops, and a reboot doesn't drop it.

The "boot loop" that was my own serial console

Oct 2026
Problem

A freshly flashed ESP32-P4 handheld seemed to reboot itself again and again, with a 30-second splash each time.

What worked

The reboots lined up exactly with each new serial connection, and the firmware's crash record showed only external resets. The board wires the USB-serial chip's DTR/RTS lines straight to the chip's EN pin, so Linux toggling them on every port open reset the board. A tiny helper now holds the port open in a state Linux leaves alone, run as a systemd service tied to that USB device.

Result

Connecting no longer resets the board. It still resets once on plug-in and when flashing, as it should.

Lesson If a board "boot-loops" only while you're watching it, suspect your own tooling first.
